About the Debt Payoff Engine

The Debt Payoff Engine calculates the speed and mathematical efficiency of eliminating liabilities. By simulating structured overpayment plans, it maps out exact timelines to escape high-interest debt traps and stop capital leakage.

• Debt Avalanche Strategy

Orders payments by highest interest rate (APR) first. Mathematically optimal, this strategy minimizes total interest paid over time and achieves complete financial freedom in the shortest possible timeframe.

• Debt Snowball Strategy

Focuses extra payments on the smallest balance first regardless of interest rate. Provides quick psychological wins by eliminating individual accounts faster to build behavioral momentum.

• Impact of Overpayments

Directing additional cash flow above minimum payments directly reduces principal balance. This creates a compounding reduction in monthly interest charges and dramatically reduces total payoff duration.
