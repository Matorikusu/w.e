About the Debt Payoff Engine

This engine structures optimal repayment schedules across multiple liabilities. By cascading available excess monthly cash flow into specific debts, it minimizes interest drag and compresses total repayment time.

• Debt Avalanche Method

Prioritizes debts by highest interest rate (APR) first while paying minimums on others. Mathematically optimal, minimizing total interest paid and clearing balances in the shortest absolute timeframe.

• Debt Snowball Method

Prioritizes debts by smallest total balance first. While mathematically paying slightly more in interest, it produces rapid psychological wins by eliminating individual debt accounts quickly.

• Payment Cascading

Once a liability is cleared, its minimum payment along with any excess budget is immediately rolled into the next target liability, accelerating payoff velocity exponentially.

Model Assumptions & Limitations

Calculations assume fixed interest rates and consistent monthly contributions. The engine does not account for variable APR increases, late payment fees, or potential balance transfer conditions. Results are for planning purposes and do not constitute formal financial counseling.
