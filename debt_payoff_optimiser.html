About the Debt Payoff Engine

This engine structures repayment schedules across multiple liabilities. By cascading available excess monthly cash flow into specific debts, it helps illustrate how repayment strategy can affect total interest and repayment time.

• Debt Avalanche Method

Prioritises debts by highest interest rate (APR) first while paying minimums on others. This generally minimises total interest when rates, minimums and payments remain constant.

• Debt Snowball Method

Prioritises debts by smallest balance first. This can create quicker individual account wins while maintaining the same overall repayment budget.

• Payment Cascading

Once a liability is cleared, its available repayment amount is redirected towards the next target liability, accelerating the repayment schedule.

Model Assumptions & Limitations

Calculations assume fixed interest rates, monthly interest accrual and consistent minimum and additional payments. The engine does not account for variable APR changes, late payment fees, promotional rates, balance transfers or lender-specific repayment rules. Results are for planning purposes only and do not constitute formal financial advice.
