About the Debt Payoff Engine

The Debt Payoff Engine models multi-liability eradication strategies. By contrasting mathematical efficiency against psychological momentum, it identifies the fastest path to debt elimination while stopping interest leakage.

• Debt Avalanche Strategy

Directs overpayments strictly to the debt with the highest interest rate (APR) while maintaining minimums across others. Mathematically optimal, it minimizes overall interest expenditure.

• Debt Snowball Strategy

Prioritises extra payments toward the account with the lowest remaining balance. By closing out individual accounts faster, it builds behavioral momentum through quick psychological wins.

• Overpayment Compounding

When an individual debt is eliminated, its minimum monthly payment is recycled into the overpayment pool ("snowballed"), accelerating the payoff rate of remaining liabilities.

Model Assumptions & Limitations

This engine assumes fixed interest rates, fixed minimum payment rules, and no additional credit drawn during the payoff period. Results are illustrative and do not constitute financial advice.
