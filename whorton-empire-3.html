How to Play

  • 1 You earn a basic £800 stipend every collection — even with no properties.
  • 2 Buy properties in Shoreditch to grow income.
  • 2 Run collections to earn from owned assets.
  • 3 Hire advisors and assign them to properties to upgrade ranks.
  • 4 Better specialty match = cheaper, faster, higher success.
  • 5 Hire managers (one per district) for income multipliers.
  • 6 Give managers bonuses — permanent boosts.
  • 7 Security reduces hostile events and disruptions.
  • 8 Fire staff carefully — severance + reputation hit.
  • 9 Dominate a district (90%) to unlock the next.
  • 10 Hidden districts unlock via net worth or dominance.
  • 11 Every property costs wages each cycle. No advisor → rank can decay.
  • 12 You age every 12 cycles. Time is the real endgame.
  • 13 Spare cash can buy Bitcoin — price moves every collection.
  • 14 Save / Export to keep progress across sessions.

Goal: build the paper empire until the numbers look real.
