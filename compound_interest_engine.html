About the Compound Interest Engine

This engine computes the future value of investments given periodic contributions and reinvested yield. It models the compounding effect where earned interest generates its own returns over extended time horizons.

• Principal vs. Interest Growth

Initial capital and ongoing deposits form the baseline principal. Over time, the exponential curve takes over as interest component outweighs personal contributions.

• Compounding Frequency

Determines how frequently accrued returns are added back into the principal balance. Higher frequencies (e.g., daily or monthly) compound total returns slightly faster.

• Capital Multiplier

Measures total return efficiency by comparing final accumulated capital directly against sum total personal contributions made across the horizon.

Model Assumptions & Limitations

Calculations assume fixed, non-fluctuating annual return rates and continuous monthly deposits without interruption. Real market returns vary annually and are subject to inflation, taxes, and management fees, which are not reflected in this baseline calculation.
