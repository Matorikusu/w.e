About the Compound Interest Engine

The Compound Interest Engine simulates long-term wealth expansion by projecting how initial capital and recurring deposits grow over time. Unlike simple interest, compounding calculates earnings on both the original principal and accumulated interest, generating exponential capital growth.

• Compounding Frequency

Determines how often accrued interest is credited back to the principal balance. More frequent compounding intervals (e.g. daily or monthly) yield slightly higher overall returns due to faster reinvestment cycles.

• Annual Step Increase

Simulates real-world salary progression or inflation adjustments by increasing your monthly deposit amount by a fixed percentage each year, significantly accelerating portfolio acceleration.

• Inflation Adjustments

Calculates the true future purchasing power of your portfolio by discounting future total balances against projected annual inflation rates.
