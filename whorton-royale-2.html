How to Play

  • 1 Buy properties with your cash.
  • 2 Start a Collection to earn income from everything you own in the current district.
  • 3 Hire Advisors to upgrade property ranks (higher rank = more money).
  • 4 Hire Managers and assign one per district to multiply collections.
  • 5 Hire Security to cut the risk of money battles and disruptions.
  • 6 Staff can only be assigned to one district. Unassign to move them.
  • 7 Give managers bonuses to permanently raise their multiplier.
  • 8 Reach 90% share in a district to dominate it and unlock the next.
  • 9 Auto-collect costs £2,500 per cycle and only runs while the page is open.
  • 10 Save to Device stores progress in this browser. Come back anytime.
