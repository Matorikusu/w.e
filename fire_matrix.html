About the FIRE Independence Engine

The FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) Matrix maps out your trajectory to financial autonomy. By balancing annual expenditure with targeted savings and portfolio yield rates, it defines the required capital baseline needed to sustain ongoing living expenses without relying on traditional earned income.

• Safe Withdrawal Rate (SWR)

The percentage of total portfolio assets withdrawn annually during retirement. A standard 4.0% SWR assumes an invested portfolio can perpetually support living expenses through historical market returns.

• Capital Multiplier

Directly derived from the Safe Withdrawal Rate. For example, a 4.0% SWR requires a net worth equivalent to 25x annual expenses ($1,000,000 portfolio for $40,000 annual burn).

• Real Post-Inflation Growth

Assumes net annual returns after accounting for inflation and investment fees, giving a conservative, true-value representation of purchasing power growth over time.
