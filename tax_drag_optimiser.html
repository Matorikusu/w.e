Investment Analysis & Overview

The Anatomy of Tax Drag: When assets are held inside a taxable General Investment Account (GIA), compounding returns are subject to annual friction from Dividend Tax and Capital Gains Tax (CGT). Even modest tax rates slice off a fraction of returns every year, creating a widening performance gap over long horizons compared to tax-sheltered accounts such as Individual Savings Accounts (ISAs).

Turnover and CGT Deferral: Capital gains tax is only triggered when investments are sold or rebalanced (realised). High turnover rates force you to pay CGT early and often, stripping away capital that could otherwise compound. Minimising portfolio turnover in a GIA helps defer CGT liabilities and preserves more long-term wealth.

The Strategic Power of Wrappers: Tax wrappers like ISAs eliminate income tax, dividend tax, and capital gains tax entirely. Directing new capital and utilizing mechanisms such as "Bed and ISA" transfers helps shield your capital from annual tax drag, ensuring 100% of your investment yield compounds uninterrupted.
