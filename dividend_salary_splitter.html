Optimal Remuneration Architecture

Combining a low salary with dividend distribution remains the most tax-efficient structure for business owners operating via limited companies in the UK.

• The Primary Threshold Strategy

Setting salary to the Personal Allowance / Primary Threshold (£12,570) allows salary to be written off as a tax-deductible company expense, reducing Corporation Tax while building State Pension qualifying years without triggering Personal Income Tax or Employee NI.

• Dividend Tax Dynamics

Dividends are paid out of post-tax profit. While not corporation-tax deductible, dividend tax rates (8.75% basic, 33.75% higher) are significantly lower than standard Income Tax rates combined with National Insurance contributions.

• Dividend Allowance & Brackets

Utilises the £500 tax-free Dividend Allowance alongside standard basic rate (£50,270 threshold) and higher rate band structures to prevent unwanted bracket creep and minimize total tax obligations.
