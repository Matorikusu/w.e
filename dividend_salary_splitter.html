Remuneration Architecture

Key tax thresholds and efficiency strategies for director-shareholders operating via a UK Limited Company.

🏛️ Optimal Director Salary Thresholds

Taking a low salary minimizes National Insurance contributions while keeping your State Pension qualifying years intact.

  • Personal Allowance £12,570 (0% Income Tax)
  • Secondary NI Threshold £9,100 (Employer NI starts)
  • Primary NI Threshold £12,570 (Employee NI starts)

📊 Corporation Tax vs Dividend Tax

Salaries are deductible expenses for Corporation Tax. Dividends are paid from post-tax profits and subject to personal dividend rates.

  • Small Profits Rate 19% (Profits up to £50k)
  • Main Rate 25% (Profits over £250k)
  • Dividend Allowance £500 tax-free per year

⚖️ Dividend Income Tax Bands

Dividends fall into your remaining personal tax bands after applying your salary and other income source totals.

  • Basic Rate (up to £50,270) 8.75% tax rate
  • Higher Rate (£50,271-£125,140) 33.75% tax rate
  • Additional Rate (>£125,140) 39.35% tax rate
