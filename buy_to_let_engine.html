About the Buy-to-Let Yield Engine

The Real Estate Matrix stress-tests physical property investments across operational friction, tax changes, and interest rate spikes. It evaluates whether a residential asset provides true risk-adjusted cashflow or hidden capital drag.

• Section 24 Tax Restriction

Under UK Section 24, individual landlords can no longer deduct mortgage interest from rental income before calculating income tax. Instead, they receive a flat 20% tax credit, severely eroding profits for higher-rate taxpayers compared to Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) Limited Companies.

• Cash-on-Cash Return (CoCR)

Measures the annual net cash income generated relative to the exact initial cash deployed (Deposit + Stamp Duty + Fees), providing a far more realistic metric for active investor capital deployment than headline gross yield.

• Interest Rate Stress-Testing

Simulates the exact impact of refinancing at higher interest rates. Standard lenders stress-test buy-to-let applications at 5.5%–7.0% interest coverage ratios (ICR) to safeguard against insolvency during monetary tightening cycles.
