Investment Analysis & Overview

Yield vs. Return on Capital Employed (ROCE): While Gross and Net Yield measure return relative to the property's total valuation, ROCE measures performance against the physical cash deployed. Leveraging through a mortgage amplifies your ROCE when rental income outpaces financing costs.

Managing Operating Friction: Profit margins in residential property depend heavily on controlling fixed friction costs. Accounting for property management fees, routine maintenance, and expected vacancy periods ensures realistic net income projections before acquisition.
