Assumptions & Methodology

Section 24 Tax Restriction (UK): For personal name Buy-to-Let properties in the UK, mortgage interest is no longer an allowable expense to deduct from rental income before tax. Instead, individual landlords pay tax on total gross revenue minus operating expenses, and receive a basic-rate 20% tax credit for mortgage interest costs. For higher-rate (40%) and additional-rate (45%) taxpayers, this creates tax liabilities that exceed real net cashflow.

US Operating Structure: Under standard IRS rules for residential rental property, mortgage interest, property taxes, insurance, and operating friction remain fully deductible expenses against gross rental income. Cap Rate measures unleveraged property performance (NOI / Purchase Price), while Cash-on-Cash Return measures leveraged yield on deployed equity.

Net Operating Yield vs. ROCE / Cash-on-Cash: Net Yield evaluates property performance prior to debt, while ROCE / Cash-on-Cash measures the performance of the actual cash capital deployed into the deal after mortgage interest and tax friction.
