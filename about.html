Overview

About the Platform

Whorton Estate is a free suite of precision engines for capital growth, tax modelling, and unit economics.

Philosophy

Built for individuals and professionals who require accurate figures without noise. Zero fluff, absolute functional utility, and completely free by design—the core tools remain open and accessible without subscription constraints.

Technical Approach & Privacy

All engines run client-side where possible to ensure your financial inputs remain private and never touch an external server. Every engine models current UK tax regulations, explicitly displays its underlying mathematical assumptions, and requires no account creation for core utility.

Operational Principles

  • No Financial Advice: Tools provide deterministic calculations, not personal advisory services.
  • No Data Monetisation: User inputs are never tracked, harvested, or sold.
  • No Friction: Zero upsell pressure, intrusive pop-ups, or artificial feature gating.

Verification & Accuracy

Our computational models are regularly audited against official HMRC rules and published UK tax schedules. Every tool clearly displays its verification date, and engines are updated immediately upon legislative policy shifts.
